# Key findings (descriptive, not causal)

- Dataset contains 1,292,954 review records across 9,560 products and 1,157,633 reviewers.
- Overall mean rating is 4.05/5; 75.0% of records have ratings of 4 or 5.
- Headphones has the largest review volume (359,334; 27.8% of records). This is review activity, not sales share.
- Wearable Technology has the lowest average rating (3.49/5); inspect its low-rating share and review volume before prioritising action.
- 74.4% of records have no brand label, limiting brand-level comparisons.
- Among records with a brand label, Bose has the highest review count (29,222); this does not establish highest sales.
- Highest review-volume month is 2016-01 (35,343 records); this reflects dataset review activity and historical coverage, not seasonality in sales.
